   GetByReceiptAsync_ImageExists_ReturnsIt (ReceiptImageServiceTests.cs:136) и
   GetByReceiptAsync_EmptyReceiptId_ThrowsArgumentException (147).
   Объяснение: private/protected методы (EnsureParticipants, ComputeBalances и т.п.) прямых
